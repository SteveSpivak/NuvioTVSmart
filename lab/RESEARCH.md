# Research

**Other television applications avoid keeping every row as a promoted document layer.** Nuvio can copy the idea (load and mount only rows near focus) without copying their code. A WebGL rewrite is out of scope.

Evidence labels: VERIFIED means the cited file or page was opened this session. INFERRED means a conclusion from that text. SPECULATIVE means not checked. Closed applications were not copied.

## What not to copy

- Do not copy Lightning, Cobalt, or Netflix renderer code. Study behavior only unless the license is compatible and the owner asks.
- Do not add `content-visibility` again. It was measured and rejected.
- Stremio addon manifests are data. None were executed.
